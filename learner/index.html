      The crew behind this path

      Three local demo agents power the path you just built. This crew runs in this page, on your device — it is a demo crew, not a live ACP hire, and no agent here chats with you, takes payment, or transacts on your behalf.

      Scout — finds & reads the posting
      Tool · Firecrawl (live build) / local text (this prep build)

      Reads the posting text end to end and pulls out the requirement phrases — tools, safety, certifications, and skills named by the employer.

      Mapper — maps text to the skill graph
      Graph · SmartCiti.X demo skill set

      Matches each requirement phrase to a skill in the graph, then checks it against the demo learner's completed skills to split covered vs. gaps.

      Coach — sequences the next 3 & explains why
      Rule · Tier first, then posting order

      Orders the gaps so prerequisites come first, picks just the next 3, and attaches a strand, tier, time estimate, and first action to each.

      Locomotion — teleport waypoints (same floor pads as in the scene; works in VR and walk mode):
      • In VR: point a controller ray at a glowing checkpoint orb, a prop token, a floor pad or the coach figure and pull the trigger (select). Thumbstick moves you, pushing the stick sideways snap-turns, floor pads teleport.
      • Gaze (no controllers): hold the centre reticle on a target for about 1.4 seconds to select it — the pointer-free route Meta's VR Glasses (spring 2027) are built around.
      • Walk mode: drag to look, W A S D / arrow keys to move, Q / E to turn, and click a marker, pad, token, coach or board row to use it.
      • Session board: the board on the apron mirrors the session in the Full Training Sessions section — pick its answer rows in VR or walk mode and the same checkpoint, hint-ladder, 3-fail-ease and Flow logic runs. A hint row sits under the answers.

        Phase 6 · Flow State

        Flow State — a derived reading from the sample log

        SmartCiti's learner-progress contract (LearnerProgressEvent) already records what a coach needs: mastery (0–1), attempts, hints_used, zpd_level, and outcome (passed / failed / gated). TradeQuest reads those fields through the SmartCiti ZPD dial (control/graph.mjs — ZPD dial, affect, hint ladder, gates) and derives one of four readings: in flow, stuck, bored risk (work below the dial), or anxious risk (work above the dial, hints climbing).

        Demo reading from sample data — not a measurement. This is an arithmetic reading over the sample log fields shown below, for the demo learners only. Not an assessment. It is not a measured emotion, not a medical or psychological assessment, and it does not detect any real learner's state.

        How the dial decides: outcome failed with attempts ≥ 3 inside/above the ZPD band → stuck — hold the gate, drop a rung on the hint ladder, offer an easier step and a coach check-in instead of advancing. Outcome passed with mastery rising inside the band → in flow — advance to the next gate. A pass far below the band with mastery already high → bored risk (raise the dial). Failures with hints climbing above the band → anxious risk (lower the dial, add support).
