How to Play — Smart Citi

Smart Citi is the card-quest face of SmartCiti.X: 939 Master Cards across 41 worlds, built from the WorldForge worlds. All 41 quest worlds were verified winnable in the local build (V1.1, verified locally). The idea of the game is the idea of the city itself, in miniature: agents operate, people govern. You play in two halves — first you operate a district, then you govern it, and the mission only counts when people enact it.

Play the Smart Citi game Browse the 41 worlds first

The game is not minted and not for sale. These are game cards only — no chain, no contract, no price. Rarity and stats are designed at design time and labelled as designed on every card; they are not drawn from play or sales data.
Local build only. Your collection, deck, and quest wins live only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, and clearing your browser storage clears your game.

1. Start with the collection

Open the game and start on the Collection tab. Every card shows its world, its kind, its designed rarity, and its three stats — Power, Build, and Cost. Search by name or description, or filter by world, kind, or rarity when you are looking for something specific. Click any card to read its full detail: its source card ID, its parent card, and — printed on the card itself — which actions are reserved for humans.

On your first visit the game grants a free Starter Deck of 30 cards. Packs hold 5 cards, are free, and are unlimited — this is a local game, so opening a pack costs nothing and buys nothing.

2. Build a legal deck — exactly 20 cards

A deck is legal only when it meets every rule below. The game checks the rules as you build and tells you plainly when the deck is not legal yet.

RuleWhat it means
Exactly 20 cardsNo more, no fewer.
Max 1 MythicOne Mythic card per deck.
Max 3 LegendaryUp to three Legendary cards per deck.
Max 4 of any one kindKinds include Zone, Agent, Advisor, Council, Seat, and the other card kinds in the collection.
At least 1 Council cardYou cannot enact a mission without a Council.
At least 2 Seat cardsA mission needs a Steward Council quorum — 2 of 3 Seats.

In the Packs & Deck tab you can auto-build a legal deck, clear it, and see your owned-card and deck counts. Click a card in the Collection to add it to the deck or remove it.

3. Run a District Quest — operate, then govern

Pick a world in the Quests tab and start the quest. You are dealt that world's real cards, and the quest runs in the game's two halves:

  1. Operate: play 1 Zone card plus any 2 Agent or Advisor cards. This is the agents' half — the district is running.
  2. Govern: then enact the mission by playing its Council card plus at least 2 of its 3 Seat cards. This is the people's half — human-reserved actions can never be played by agents alone.

Finish before your turns run out. Your score is the total Power of the cards you played. A quest is complete when the quorum is reached: Council plus Seats, with the log line the game was built around — agents operated; people governed.

4. Climb the Campaign

The Campaign tab maps the game onto the SmartCiti.X learner structure — strands by tiers: Fundamentals → Applied → Mastery — with the Holodeck as your home district. Win quests to climb: each tier you clear raises your rank, and higher tiers give you fewer turns — 10 turns at Fundamentals, 8 at Applied, 6 at Mastery. Win any 3 world quests to advance from a tier. Quest wins are saved locally, in this browser only.
