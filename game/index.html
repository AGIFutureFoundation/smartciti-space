Deck rules

🗺️ District Quest — Build the Citi

Pick a world. You are dealt that world's real cards. Operate: play 1 Zone + any 2 Agents/Advisors. Govern: then enact the mission by playing its Council card + at least 2 of its 3 Seat cards (Steward Council 2-of-3 quorum — human-reserved actions can never be played by agents alone). Finish before turns run out. Score = total Power of cards played.
