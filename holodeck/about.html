About the Holodeck — read this, then step in

The Holodeck is SmartCiti.X's bundled 3D training scene: the IWSDK Training Centre, served from this site. Its own page is a WebXR scene shell — it opens straight into the 3D scene and carries no prose of its own — so this page is the landing around it: what the scene is, what to expect inside, and where it sits among the worlds, before you open it.

What is inside

The Training Centre is ten procedural 3D zones on a shared 36×24 shell, with no downloaded assets — the scene is generated by the bundled build rather than loaded from an asset library. The scene greets you with a welcome panel and works through direct interaction: its built-in labels teach the three moves the scene is organised around — grab, select, and spin — and a trade-path heads-up display ties what you handle in the scene back to the TradeQuest training path.

The Holodeck is also a destination from the training modules: several TradeQuest modules link into it by zone, including the electrical, HVAC, and dental screening outreach scenarios, so a station you work on the flat site has a room you can step into here.

Where it sits — anchor 39.846, −86.622

In the WorldForge-Browser build, the Holodeck is one of six anchored worlds, at anchor 39.846, −86.622. It is the home district of the Smart Citi game campaign — the district every player starts from — and the first door in the comic, Smart Citi Issue #1 — First Day in the Holodeck. The full anchor set and all 41 worlds are listed on the worlds index.

The Holodeck scene descriptor is also the piece served by the fabric host that feeds the RP1 attach pipeline, linked from the hub. The in-RP1 attach completes once the public map service deploys — until then, the way into the Holodeck is this site, the VR entry, and the WorldForge VR entry at www.agi.lease. No worlds are live in RP1 yet.

A sensible order to visit

  1. Run a module first — Safety Procedures, then a trade scenario — so the zones have a task attached to them.
  2. Read the guide's fifth step — the SmartCiti.X guide places the Holodeck at the end of the five-step walkthrough for a reason.
  3. Then step in — open the Holodeck scene. It loads the bundled IWSDK build directly; give the scene a moment, and use the in-scene welcome panel and the grab / select / spin labels as your controls guide.

If the 3D scene does not suit your device, nothing is lost: every training step the Holodeck hosts is also reachable in the flat learner app and module runner.
