The Worlds — a WorldForge index

SmartCiti.X is the home of the WorldForge worlds project: 41 worlds in the public WorldForge-Browser build. The same 41 worlds carry the Smart Citi game — 939 Master Cards across 41 worlds, all 41 quest worlds verified winnable in the local build. This page is the index the site was missing: the six anchored worlds with their coordinates, the full list of 41, and the doors into each way of visiting them.

RP1 status, plainly: RP1 CompanyId 130 is confirmed. No worlds are live in RP1 yet. The worlds below are visited in the browser build and played in the local game build — not in RP1.
What SmartCiti.X is: W3 LLC Series 10 — listed as S10 SmartCiti.X in the W3 LLC internal series list (April 2026). That list is an internal planning document; a per-series designation is not on file. SmartCiti.X is proposed, not incorporated / funded / offered.

Three ways into the worlds

  1. Walk them: the WorldForge-Browser Earth view carries all 41 worlds in the public build today, and the WorldForge VR entry point is www.agi.lease.
  2. Play them: the Smart Citi game turns each world into a District Quest — operate with Zone, Agent, and Advisor cards, then govern by Steward Council quorum.
  3. Train in one: the Holodeck and the learner VR entry are the SmartCiti.X training faces of the same world set, tied to the TradeQuest modules.

RP1 CompanyId 130 is confirmed, and the Holodeck fabric host that feeds the RP1 pipeline is linked from the hub. The in-RP1 attach completes once the public map service deploys. No worlds are live in RP1 yet.
