What the Holodeck is

A spatial city simulation with a job to do: turn a city's working systems into rooms a learner can practise in, and turn the network that built it into a structure a visitor can walk.

A city, simulated

The Holodeck district is the home district of the Smart Citi game campaign — the district every player starts from — and the first door in the comic Smart Citi Issue #1 — First Day in the Holodeck. The city is the curriculum: its systems are the lessons.

WorldForge underneath

WorldForge authors the worlds; the Holodeck is one anchored world among 41. The same district appears on the Earth view, in the worlds index, in the game's 939 Master Cards across 41 worlds (the game is not minted and not for sale), and in the fabric descriptor the RP1 pipeline reads.

The RP1 fabric

RP1 is a registry: developers self-host a map service and attach their fabric. The SmartCiti.X map-service package is built and the Holodeck scene descriptor is served today — the attach itself waits on the public map-service deploy. Until then, the doors above are the way in, and this site says so plainly wherever RP1 is mentioned.

A sensible order to visit

  1. Run a module first — Safety Procedures, then a trade scenario — so the zones have a task attached to them.
  2. Read the guide's fifth step — the SmartCiti.X guide places the Holodeck at the end of its five-step walkthrough for a reason.
  3. Then step in — open the Holodeck scene, and let the welcome panel and the grab / select / spin labels be your controls guide.
