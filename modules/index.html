TradeQuest — Union Training Modules

Ten modular training environments. Each module is one trade scenario: read the objectives, work the five steps in order at their stations, and check each step off as you complete it. Your progress is saved on this device only — nothing is stored on a server or sent anywhere.

Demo mode — sample training content. Simulation only — NOT certification, licensure, or an apprenticeship credential.
SmartCiti.X is proposed, not incorporated / funded / offered. Louisiana + Indiana training sites are proposed/planned — no site, city, or address has been named. The 111-hall roster in the SmartCiti.X bundle is an in-repo list; 0 of 111 halls are signed off by a named practitioner — a named hall is not a partner, and trade organisations named in module sources are alignment references, not partners.
